# Train Takes — Suno Prompt

A Thomas the Tank Engine-inspired theme song for Nick Sellers / Train Takes (@traintakes).

---

## Style of Music

British children's TV theme, jaunty brass band march, in the spirit of the Thomas the Tank Engine theme. Bouncy 4/4 at ~118 BPM, key of C major. Lead instruments: trumpet fanfare, tuba bassline walking on the beat, trombone slides, glockenspiel sparkle, snare drum march, tambourine. Upbeat narrator-style male lead vocal, cheery and slightly cheeky, light British accent. Big children's-choir backing vocals on the chorus shouting "TRAIN TAKES!". Sound effects throughout: train whistle toots, steam hisses, "all aboard!" calls, station bell. Tight, punchy production. Singalong, family-friendly, tongue-in-cheek. Fade-in steam intro, big brass outro with whistle.

---

## Notes

- **Reference track:** Thomas the Tank Engine theme (the original Mike O'Donnell / Junior Campbell brass-band version, not the later orchestral one).
- **If Suno keeps drifting away from brass band:** add `[no synths]` and `[no electric guitar]` to the style box.
- **For a punchier 60-second cut:** drop Verse 3 and the Bridge, keep Verses 1–2, the Pre-chorus, and two Choruses.
- **Voice direction tweak:** if the lead sounds too American, add "Ringo Starr narrator energy" to the style line.
